Honored to receive a **2026 Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) Young Faculty Award!** Our project is titled **"CMOS-Addressable DNA Random Access Memory."**

The project aims to combine the durability and density of molecular data storage with the electronic programmability of silicon. Our team will explore a hybrid memory in which silicon chips control how information stored in DNA is written, accessed, modified, and erased, moving DNA storage beyond long-term archiving toward electronically addressable memory.

The award provides up to **$719,358 in research support over two years**, with a conditional third year.
