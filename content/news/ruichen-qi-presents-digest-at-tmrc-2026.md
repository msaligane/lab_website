Ruichen Qi presented the digest **"An AI-Driven Co-Design Framework for CMOS and Emerging Memory Devices"** at the 37th Magnetic Recording Conference ([TMRC 2026](https://tmrc2026.ucsd.edu/)), held at UC San Diego.

TMRC is a focused conference on magnetic memory, storage, and recording technologies. The presentation was well received by the community, sparking great discussions on AI-driven co-design for emerging memory devices.
