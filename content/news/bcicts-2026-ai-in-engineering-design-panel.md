Mehdi Saligane joined the IEEE Women in Engineering and Young Professionals panel, "AI in Engineering Design: Opportunities, Challenges, and the Road Ahead." The panel brought together researchers and engineers from academia, industry, and startups to discuss where AI can help chip designers, what it takes to trust AI tools in practice, and what skills the next generation of engineers will need.

Thank you to Sadayuki Yoshitomi, the BCICTS 2026 Technical Program Committee Chair, for the invitation!
