CHIPS Alliance featured Prof. Mehdi Saligane in its Developer Spotlight series,
highlighting his work to rethink both how chips are designed and who gets to
design them.

The spotlight explores the lab's work across AI-assisted design, open-source
electronic design automation, analog and mixed-signal automation,
hardware-software co-design for AI, and secure hardware. It also discusses the
open infrastructure and reproducible engineering practices needed to make
custom silicon more accessible.
